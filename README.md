## Pascha vs. Easter Date Calculator

Orthodox Pascha and Western Easter for any year from 1583 to 4099, the gap
between them, and the full movable cycle for both traditions.

Live at **https://tools.orthodoxsnake.com**

### Accuracy

Both Paschalions verified against 21 published anchor years and checked for
structural correctness across all 2,517 valid years (1583–4099). Two
independent implementations produce identical output across that range.

Always defer to your own jurisdiction's published calendar for liturgical
planning.
